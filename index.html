About

I'm a mechanical engineering graduate student at Johns Hopkins, in the SciPhy Lab, where I work on high-resolution tactile sensing and learning-based methods for contact-rich manipulation. Right now I'm building an evaluation system that compares tactile sensors across a range of dexterous tasks.

Before Hopkins I studied mechanical engineering at DTU in New Delhi and did research on inverse kinematics at IIT Delhi and inverted-pendulum platforms at IHFC. I'm aiming toward a PhD in visuotactile manipulation.
