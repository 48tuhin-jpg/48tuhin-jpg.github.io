About

I’m a mechanical engineering MSE student at Johns Hopkins University, conducting research in the SciPhy Lab, where I work on tactile sensing and learning-based methods for contact-rich manipulation. Right now I’m building an evaluation system that compares tactile sensors across a range of dexterous tasks. I'm also looking into ways robots can use tactile sensing to manipulate deformable bodies, by learning the underlying dynamics via active tactile probing.

Before Hopkins I studied mechanical engineering at DTU in New Delhi. I began my research journey in robotics at IIT Delhi, as a research intern under Prof. S K Saha and Dr. Rajeev G Chittawadigi. I was then selected as a READY intern at IHFC, where I did product developement and research in control methods for underactuated systems. I’m currently aiming toward a PhD, and interested in working in Visuo-Tactile manipulation.
